Part 1 – Executive Summary, Vision & Platform Foundation
1. Executive Summary
1.1 Overview
Qorrect
 Operations Management Platform (QOMP) is an internal enterprise platform designed to centralize operational processes across different departments within 
Qorrect
.
The platform initially combines Customer Relationship Management (CRM), Customer Success Management, and Environment Management into a single integrated solution while providing an architecture that allows future expansion into additional business domains such as HR, Finance, Inventory, Asset Management, and other operational modules.
Instead of maintaining multiple disconnected systems, QOMP provides a unified platform where users can manage customers, operational environments, deployments, support activities, meetings, tasks, reports, and integrations from one centralized application.
1.2 Business Problem
Currently, business information is distributed across multiple tools and manual processes.
Examples include:
Customer information
Customer Success activities
Environment configurations
Jira tickets
Meetings
Deployment tracking
Certificates
Documentation
This separation creates several challenges:
Duplicate data
Difficult tracking
Poor visibility
Manual communication
Limited reporting
Lost operational history
QOMP addresses these issues by becoming the single source of truth for all operational activities.
1.3 Product Vision
Create a modular enterprise platform that enables every department to perform its daily operations from a unified system while maintaining security, scalability, and flexibility.
The platform should reduce manual work, improve collaboration, simplify reporting, and provide complete operational visibility.
1.4 Product Goals
The platform aims to achieve the following objectives:
Centralize operational information.
Improve customer management.
Improve customer success workflows.
Simplify environment management.
Track operational activities.
Integrate with external systems.
Increase visibility across departments.
Support future expansion without redesigning the platform.

2. Product Principles
The following principles apply to every module within the platform.
2.1 Modular Architecture
Every business function must exist as an independent module.
Examples:
Customer Management
Customer Success
Environment Management
HR
Finance
Inventory
Modules should communicate through shared platform services while remaining independently maintainable.
2.2 Security First
Every action inside the platform must respect:
User Role
Module Membership
Permissions
Users should never access information outside their authorized scope.
2.3 Reusable Platform Services
Instead of rebuilding features for every module, common platform services will be shared.
Examples include:
Attachments
Comments
Notifications
Timeline
Search
Audit Logs
Tags
Favorites
2.4 Scalability
The platform must support adding new modules without requiring major architectural changes.
2.5 Single Source of Truth
Every business entity should have one authoritative record inside the platform.
External systems should synchronize with this record instead of replacing it.

The Customer Success Module enables Customer Success teams to proactively manage customer relationships, monitor customer health, schedule engagements, track follow-up activities, manage renewals, and ensure long-term customer satisfaction.
